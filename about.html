Meet Dr. Elsaadi

Decades of experience. Care that still feels personal.

Dr. Magdi Elsaadi is a board-certified Internal Medicine physician who has cared for adults in Loma Linda and San Bernardino since 1991.

His approach

A physician who knows the story behind the chart.

For Dr. Elsaadi, a useful visit begins before a diagnosis is discussed. It begins with listening carefully, understanding what has changed and giving patients enough room to ask the questions they came to ask.

That approach matters most over time. Primary care is not only about treating today’s concern; it is about recognizing patterns, coordinating care and helping each patient make informed decisions as their health evolves.

The result is experienced adult medicine with the continuity and attention of an independent practice.

Professional journey

Rooted in San Bernardino.

After completing postgraduate medical training, Dr. Elsaadi built his clinical career in the Inland Empire. He spent approximately 25 years with San Bernardino Medical Group before establishing his independent practice in 2020.

His work has also included physician leadership, peer review, credentialing and quality-improvement responsibilities at St. Bernardine Medical Center.
