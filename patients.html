Your first visit

Arrive prepared, not overwhelmed.

Office staff will confirm the final requirements when your appointment is scheduled.

  • Photo identificationA current government-issued ID.
  • Insurance cardYour current medical plan card and applicable referral information.
  • Medication listMedication names, doses and how often you take them.
  • Relevant recordsRecent results or records the office has asked you to bring.

Using Healow

Keep private health information in the secure portal.

The practice’s public website does not collect symptoms, medical records, medication requests or insurance documents. Use Healow for supported clinical communication and account-specific information.

Portal tools shown for this practice include health-record access, communication, appointment management, test results and medication/refill functions. Available options can vary by account.

Do not use Healow for emergencies. For a medical emergency, call 911. For a time-sensitive concern, call the office.
