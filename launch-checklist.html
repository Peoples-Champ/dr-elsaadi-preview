Pre-launch verification checklist

This prototype keeps building without inventing uncertain facts. Items marked “Verify” should be approved by Irene or Dr. Elsaadi before the site replaces the current public website.

  • Confirmed
    Portal platform

    Healow/eClinicalWorks; current web portal and secure pre-registration URLs identified.

  • Confirmed
    Appointment workflow

    Patients submit a request. The office follows up in Healow or by phone; this is not instant self-scheduling.

  • Confirmed
    Main phone and fax

    Phone (909) 881-9918 and fax (909) 881-9927 appear consistently in current institutional listings.

  • Confirmed
    Primary office address

    Confirmed by the practice: 2015 N Waterman Ave, Suite A, San Bernardino, CA 92404.

  • Verify
    Office hours

    Current Optum listing shows 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; other directories show 8:30 AM–5:00 PM. Confirm exact daily and lunch hours.

  • Verify
    Accepting new patients

    Current Optum and Healthgrades profiles say yes, but the office should approve this claim for the website.

  • Verify
    Insurance and medical groups

    Obtain the current plan/network list. Avoid broad carrier-level claims without exact plan confirmation.

  • Verify
    Hospital affiliations

    St. Bernardine is supported by current profiles. Confirm Community Hospital and Ballard Rehabilitation before publishing.

  • Verify
    Languages

    Current listings disagree. Confirm languages spoken by Dr. Elsaadi and languages supported by staff.

  • Verify
    Portal functions

    Confirm which Healow tools the office has enabled: results, refills, billing, telehealth, messaging and appointment management.

  • Verify
    Same-day and telehealth availability

    The old site advertises both. Confirm the current policy and eligible visit types.

  • Verify
    After-hours instructions

    Provide the exact directions patients should follow when the office is closed.

  • Verify
    Photography and video

    Schedule updated doctor, staff, reception, exam-room, exterior, entrance and parking photos plus the physician introduction video.

  • Verify
    Testimonials and awards

    Approve the specific quotations, attributions, leadership wording and community-service recognition before public launch.
