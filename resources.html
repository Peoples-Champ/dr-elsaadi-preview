Before you contact the office.

How do I get access to Healow?

After your first visit, provide your email address to the office to receive an invitation. Use practice code FGCJBD. New patients schedule the first visit by phone.

What if I cannot sign in?

Try Healow’s account-recovery tools. Call the office if you still need help. Do not send your password or medical information through ordinary email.

Can I pay a bill online?

The practice does not currently offer online bill pay. Call the office with billing questions.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Photo ID, your insurance card and all current medications. Contact the office in advance if previous records or labs need to be requested.
