Insurance

A carrier name is not the same as a confirmed network.

Participation can vary by the exact plan, medical group and network. Before scheduling, call the office and your health plan to confirm that Dr. Elsaadi is in-network for your specific coverage.

Have your insurance card available. The member-services number on the back of the card is the best place to confirm benefits and any referral requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Before you contact the office.

Can I book an exact appointment time online?

The Healow workflow accepts appointment requests. The office reviews the request and follows up to complete scheduling.

Where should I send a refill request?

Use the refill option available in Healow or call the office. Do not send medication details through a general website form.

Does the practice offer telehealth?

Telehealth is listed as available for appropriate visits. Call or request an appointment so the office can determine whether a virtual visit fits your needs.

What if I need help logging in?

Use the account-recovery option on Healow. If that does not solve the issue, call the front desk for help.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a response through the patient portal.
