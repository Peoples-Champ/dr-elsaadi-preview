Adult medicine

Care organized around the whole person.

Preventive care, everyday medical needs and long-term condition management—guided by one experienced physician who understands how the pieces fit together.

Primary & Preventive Care

Routine adult care focused on prevention, early detection and a clear view of your overall health.

  • Annual wellness and routine physical visits
  • Age-appropriate screening review
  • Medication and health-risk review
  • Preventive counseling

Diabetes & Metabolic Health

Ongoing medical support for common metabolic conditions and the risks that often travel together.

  • Diabetes and prediabetes
  • Blood sugar monitoring
  • Cholesterol management
  • Coordinated long-term follow-up

Blood Pressure & Cardiovascular Health

Evaluation and continuing care for blood-pressure and cardiovascular risk concerns.

  • Hypertension
  • Heart-disease risk assessment
  • Medication monitoring
  • Lifestyle and prevention planning

Thyroid & Endocrine Conditions

Evaluation and management of common thyroid concerns with attention to symptoms, testing and follow-up.

  • Underactive thyroid
  • Overactive thyroid
  • Thyroid testing review
  • Ongoing medication management

Arthritis, Neuropathy & Pain

Medical evaluation for pain and nerve symptoms, with referral coordination when specialty care is needed.

  • Arthritis and joint concerns
  • Back pain and sciatica
  • Neuropathy
  • Chronic-pain evaluation

General Adult Medicine

A familiar first point of contact for common illness, changing symptoms and medical questions.

  • Everyday illness
  • Medical consultation
  • Care coordination
  • Telehealth when appropriate

When you are not sure where to begin.

Primary care is often the right first call. The office can help determine whether a concern belongs with Dr. Elsaadi, should be handled through telehealth or requires urgent or specialty care.

Medical emergency? Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use the patient portal for urgent or emergency concerns.
